WELCOME TO THE COURTYARD

One board. Three possibilities.

Each live round gives you 30 seconds to find the strongest move. Look for the A, B, and C labels on the board, then choose your move.

  1. Take in the position.The stone below the board tells you whose turn it is.
  2. Make your choice.Click a move or press A, B, or C. You can change your vote until time runs out.
  3. Learn, then begin again.The best move is revealed after each round. A correct answer earns the seconds remaining when you chose it.

Your points stay with you on this device. Open courtyard chat to talk through the position with other players.
